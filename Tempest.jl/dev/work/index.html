A launch's work

Work is one launch's work, as an implementation states it for Tempest to price.

What a launch states

fieldwhat it states
bytesbytes moved
rereadbytes it reads again (what its blocks read of an argument beyond the first time)
passedbytes it passes to or takes from another of its claim's launches (partial results in a workspace, as a split sum's parts)
flops, factorflops of products whose factors are of type factor
recomputedflops of folds it recomputes inside them
blocks, splitits blocks, and whether they split the sums among them
appliedthe scalar functions it applies elementwise, each with the number of positions it is applied at
sweptfunctions applied inside a product's sweep, to its factors
innerfunctions applied inside a fold the sweep recomputes, to that fold's tiles
stepsthe steps a block takes, where blocks sweep tiles one after another
step_runswhat a block loads at a step: of each load, the bytes it reads in one run, contiguous, paired with the bytes it reads in the step
steppedthe functions the steps compute on what they loaded, each with the number of steps it is applied in along their chain
refold_stepsthe steps apart in which a block recomputes a fold whose body is not bilinear along its axis

How it is priced

Tempest prices the bytes on its package's memory, those read again from the cache, those passed within the cache where they fit it, as the launch that takes them finds them there, the flops on the line its package reaches for products of that class, and each function on the time it measured for it on the device (see seconds).

Functions applied inside a product's sweep, to its factors, are swept: each step's small tile of them stalls the sweep, and they cost many times what they cost streamed; those applied inside a fold the sweep recomputes, to that fold's tiles, are inner, at a factor of their own.

Sweeps in steps

A launch whose blocks sweep tiles one after another, waiting for what each step loads before the next, states the steps a block takes, what a block loads at a step (step_runs: of each load, the bytes it reads in one run, contiguous, paired with the bytes it reads in the step), and the functions the steps compute on what they loaded, each with the number of steps it is applied in along their chain (stepped): a step takes its latency and its bytes at what one block reaches, however few positions it covers, and a sweep of many narrow steps takes that many times as long, whatever bytes it moves.

Tempest prices a step on its package's memory and each function on the time it measured for it in a step (see seconds). A launch that states no steps, or one, is priced by its bytes, flops and functions alone, as a product's sweep is by its line.

Folds recomputed

A fold a launch recomputes where it reads it, its flops recomputed, a block sweeps in steps of its own, before the sweep or inside each step the fold moves along: a product's steps, among the sweep's, where its body is bilinear along its axis, and otherwise refold_steps apart, combining its body elementwise, which wait on their own loads one after another with the rest of the block's work, however the sweep hides its bytes; Tempest prices each as a step of the sweep.
